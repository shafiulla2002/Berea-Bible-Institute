Biblical Languages Beginner 3 Credits

Introduction to Biblical Greek (Koine)

Master foundational Koine Greek grammar, vocabulary, noun declensions, and verb conjugations to translate the New Testament directly from original manuscripts.

Lead Instructor Dr. Paul Apostle
Course Length 12 Weeks
Instruction Format Online Interactive
Upcoming Cohort June 1, 2026

Course Description & Scope

This course provides a comprehensive introduction to the grammar, syntax, and vocabulary of the Greek New Testament.

Academic Notice: This course fulfills the core biblical language requirement for the Certificate in Biblical Studies and Diploma in Pastoral Ministry.
