ANSWERS & GUIDELINES

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about enrollment, tuition, and study formats.

Are courses conducted online, on campus, or hybrid? +

Bethesda Bible Institute offers interactive live classes over Zoom/Google Meet, as well as flexible self-paced video lectures and hybrid intensive weekend modules. All live sessions are recorded and archived with proctored lecture materials in our Student Portal.

Can I audit a course without completing formal examinations? +

Yes! Students may choose the Audit option during enrollment for personal enrichment. Auditors attend lectures and receive study notes but are exempt from graded papers, quizzes, and final examinations.

What financial assistance or scholarships are available? +

Through the generous support of our partner churches, we offer pastoral ministry subsidies, missionary grants, and merit scholarships. Enter promo code BIBLE2026 at checkout for an immediate 20% tuition scholarship.

What is the registration process for new students? +

New candidates complete the online student registration form, upload academic credentials, select desired courses, and review our academic honor code. Upon enrollment confirmation, login credentials for the Student Portal are generated immediately.
